> **In brief:**
> 1. There is no single best business creation support, but an ecosystem of actors intervening at different stages of the journey.
> 2. Bpifrance Création helps upstream to frame the project, chambers of commerce and craft chambers handle formalities, honour loan networks fund seed stage, and the accountant handles structural choices and follow-up after registration.
> 3. On the accounting side, In Extenso supports 8,000 entrepreneurs each year with its dedicated In Extenso Business Creation and Takeover line and a 10-step method, and 90% of businesses it supports survive the first three years.
> 4. Combining these actors rather than choosing one reduces the risk of legal structure errors and administrative oversights, and ensures follow-up after registration.

The question of best business creation support rests on a misunderstanding. It assumes one actor covers the entire journey, when reality shows the opposite. Each actor intervenes at a moment in the entrepreneur's lifecycle, with a distinct expertise.

## Who does what in business creation support in France

The ecosystem divides into four families.

### Information and guidance

Bpifrance Création is the entry point. Free, its portal offers comprehensive documentation and guides you to the right contacts. You clarify your project there, test its viability on paper, explore your market type and positioning questions. This tool is especially useful for entrepreneurs starting alone, without external advice, who need to structure their thinking before spending money. It is an information and guidance tool, completed afterwards by individual support.

### Local support and formalities

Chambers of commerce and industry (CCI) cover commercial and industrial sectors, craft chambers (CMA) cover crafts. Both offer training, individual meetings and help with administrative files, especially registration at the business register or craft register and obtaining your [company registration certificate](/en/blog/company-registration-kbis/). BGE, an associative network, also supports entrepreneurs throughout the project, with business plan advice, mentoring and training. These actors are often close to you and know local context, regional aid, sector specifics. For craft workers and traders especially, this is valuable startup support, completed afterwards by accounting and tax follow-up.

### Seed funding

Initiative France and Réseau Entreprendre work on an honour loan model, granted interest-free with no security, combined with mentoring from experienced entrepreneurs. The most useful effect is not always the money lent, it is the leverage effect with banks. An honour loan strengthens equity and often unlocks bank credit that the entrepreneur would struggle to get alone. ADIE finances through microcredit entrepreneurs excluded from standard banking circuits, particularly job-seekers or those in precarious situations. France Active works on projects with social or territorial impact, combining funding and guarantee models.

Distinguish support from funding. These actors are complementary, not competitive. An entrepreneur can borrow from Initiative France while being supported by a chamber and advised by an accountant.

### Intermediate forms and sector-specific schemes

Some sectors have specific support. France Travail offers dedicated support to job-seekers starting or taking over a business, with business creation loans and personalised follow-up.

### The accountant, for structural choices and follow-up

The accountant intervenes when decisions are made that shape the business for years, then stays at your side once activity launches. Choosing your [legal structure, SARL or SAS](/en/blog/choosing-sas-sarl-legal-structure/), sole trader or company, determines taxation, the director's social regime and capacity to welcome partners. Forecasts determine bank approval. And it is after registration that questions arise on VAT, payroll or director compensation. Some firms have structured dedicated offers for entrepreneurs, like In Extenso with its In Extenso Business Creation and Takeover line.

## Building your own journey

Rather than choosing one actor, chain them in order.

1. Start with Bpifrance Création, to structure your project using free resources and clarify vocabulary.
2. Engage local networks according to your activity, BGE for personalised support, or chambers for specific formalities.
3. Find funding in the right place according to your profile, honour loan, microcredit or guarantee.
4. Settle structural choices with an [accounting firm](/en/blog/best-accounting-firm-france/), legal structure, taxation, forecasts that secure funding.
5. Keep one single contact for what comes after, because it is after registration that critical questions arise.

## Focus on In Extenso, dedicated business creation support

In Extenso has made business creation a separate service line, In Extenso Business Creation and Takeover.

### Specialist advisers in each office

In each office, entrepreneurs meet a creation adviser who plays the role of counsellor, coach and mentor. The network has 45 specialists in creation and takeover, operates from 280 offices across France and supports 8,000 entrepreneurs each year. According to In Extenso, 90% of businesses it supports survive the first three years.

### A 10-step method, from idea to launch

The approach follows a 10-step method, tailored to your needs:

1. from idea to project
2. assessing your skills and fit for the project
3. commercial and technical viability
4. future business organisation, including legal form and tax and social options
5. financial study and forecast budget
6. identifying available startup aid
7. writing the creation file, the business plan
8. preparing meetings with banks
9. legal work and registration formalities
10. setting up administration and accounting, with dashboard tools.

### Creation, takeover and franchising

Support covers creation, but also business takeover and franchise setup, across any sector, craft, commerce, professions or agriculture. Once the business launches, the same firm takes over for accounting, payroll, taxation and legal work, and the business owner has access to Inexweb, In Extenso's management portal. For business law and tax questions, articles of association, partnership agreements or tax structuring, accountants work with In Extenso Avocats, the group's business law network, with 50 legal professionals including tax lawyers. To test your project before launching, In Extenso also offers an online quiz.

## Three families of financial aid

Do not confuse support with financial aid. These schemes fall into three families and often combine.

Direct financial aid, the honour loan from Initiative France and Réseau Entreprendre, interest-free with no security, and ADIE microcredit.

Tax reliefs and social schemes. ACRE partially exempts from social charges at startup. ARCE lets a job-seeker receive part of their rights as capital rather than monthly payments.

Guarantees, France Active and Bpifrance facilitate access to bank credit without lending directly.

France Travail also offers dedicated support to job-seekers starting or taking over a business.
