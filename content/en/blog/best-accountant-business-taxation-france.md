Taxation is one area where the role of the chartered accountant is most needed. For most business owners, it is first about meeting deadlines and avoiding mistakes. But sound tax management goes further. It means making, at the right time, choices that fit your business and personal situation, within the law. Here is what to expect from a chartered accountant on taxation and how to choose one.

## What business taxation covers

### Tax reporting, the foundation

Every business must meet regular declaration obligations:

- VAT declarations, monthly, quarterly or annually depending on the regime;
- profit declaration and tax return;
- corporate income tax or individual income tax depending on the legal structure, hence the importance of choosing between [SAS or SARL](/en/blog/choosing-sas-sarl-legal-structure/);
- [local business tax (CFE)](/en/blog/cfe-local-business-tax-france/) and, where applicable, tax on added value.

These declarations are at the heart of the chartered accountant's role. Their reliability depends on sound accounting and a clear audit trail, especially for VAT.

### Tax advisory, the added value

Beyond declarations, tax advisory helps the owner make the right choices:

- choosing your tax and VAT regime at startup or when a threshold changes;
- deciding between compensation and dividends;
- anticipating the tax impact of an investment, acquisition or restructuring;
- preparing for a sale or succession;
- assistance if audited by the tax authority. The various providers are listed in the [ranking of the best tax advisory firms](/en/blog/best-tax-advisory-firms-france-2026/).

## Criteria for choosing your chartered accountant on taxation

1. **Check that they master your regime.** Micro, simplified standard, standard, individual income tax or corporate income tax, sectoral regimes, each situation has its rules.
2. **Ask whether they offer options, not just declarations.** Good tax advice shows you costed options before a decision.
3. **Ask them about tax audits.** Who will assist you if the tax authority audits, and under what terms.
4. **Think of the owner as much as the business.** Compensation, wealth, succession, the owner's personal taxation is linked to their business taxation.
5. **Look at the firm's breadth.** A firm combining taxation, legal and wealth management means one fewer advisor to manage.
