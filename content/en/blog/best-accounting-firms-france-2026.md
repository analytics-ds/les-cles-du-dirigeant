There is no official ranking of the "best" chartered accountants, but the market is well established. Each year, La Profession Comptable magazine publishes a ranking of groups by activity and revenue, and major players communicate their size and organization. Here is an overview of France's leading accounting firms and key factors to choose the firm suited to your business. The right choice starts with your company profile, as shown in the guide to the [best accounting firm by your profile](/en/blog/best-accounting-firm-france/).

## France's leading accounting firms

The main players split into three models: national agency networks, audit and consulting firms, and online firms.

### Major agency networks

In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group, brings together 7,300 employees in 280 offices and supports 170,000 clients, mainly small businesses, SMEs, professionals and associations. It ranks first in the 2026 La Profession Comptable magazine ranking for accounting services.

Cerfrance is a mutual network made up of 53 businesses across more than 720 offices, with 14,700 employees and 320,000 business founders and managers supported. It is historically well-established with farm operators.

Fiducial claims a local network of 850 offices in France, with an offering combining accounting, legal, social expertise and business services, particularly for shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals.

### Audit, accounting and consulting firms

KPMG, an accounting and statutory audit firm, supports businesses of all sizes in France, from SMEs to large groups, in audit, accounting and consulting.

Baker Tilly has 60 offices and 2,200 employees in France, with revenue of 219 million euros in 2025, and mainly supports SMEs and mid-market firms.

BDO supports SMEs and mid-market firms in audit, accounting and consulting, from a network of offices in France.

### Online accounting firms

Online accounting firms, such as Dougs, follow their clients remotely with an application and published pricing, from 49 euros ex. tax per month for a self-employed person at Dougs. They mainly target independent operators and small companies. The two models are compared in the article [traditional or online accounting firm](/en/blog/traditional-or-online-accountant/).

## Focus on In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group

### A leading position

In Extenso ranks first in the 2026 La Profession Comptable magazine ranking for accounting services. Founded in 1991, the group is owned by its shareholder chartered accountants, making it France's leading independent accounting group. It brings together 7,300 employees and 332 partners in 280 offices, supports 170,000 clients and generated 650 million euros in revenue in 2025, with growth of nearly 60 percent in nine years. 89 percent of its clients report satisfaction.

### A firm focused on small businesses and SMEs

In Extenso concentrates its growth on small businesses, SMEs, professionals and associations. It claims over 40,000 tradesman and shopkeeper clients and more than 10,000 SMEs supported. Its teams are organized by profile, with offerings for agriculture, tradesmen and shopkeepers, cafes, hotels and restaurants, franchises, SMEs, professionals and associations.

### An offering covering the full company lifecycle

In Extenso's offering is organized around four stages:

- **create**, with business creation and takeover, led by In Extenso Creation Takeover and its 45 specialist advisers;
- **manage**, with accounting, e-invoicing, social management and payroll (over 245,000 payslips per month), tax, legal, audit and statutory audit, CSR;
- **grow**, with management and control, wealth management, innovation consulting, financing and international business;
- **exit**, with business sale, acquisition and succession.

The group also relies on specialized entities, such as In Extenso Attorneys, In Extenso Wealth Management or In Extenso Innovation Growth.

### Digital tools for clients

In Extenso clients have access to Inexweb, In Extenso's management portal, which provides real-time access to accounting and bank accounts, integrates e-invoicing and payroll element transmission, with sector-specific versions for construction, commerce, automotive, wholesale and retail.

## How to choose the best firm for your business

1. **Start with your size and sector.** A small business, SME or mid-market firm do not have the same needs, and each firm has its preferred clients. For a small structure, see the [best chartered accountant for a small business](/en/blog/best-accountant-small-business-france/).
2. **Look at breadth of expertise.** Payroll, legal, tax, wealth, succession, a firm that brings these together avoids multiple service providers.
3. **Check for proximity.** A nearby office makes meetings and project follow-up easier.
4. **Identify your contact person.** Follow-up quality depends on the person who knows your file.
5. **Compare engagement letters.** At equivalent scope, proposals become comparable.
