## How to choose your chartered accountant in Lyon

A few questions allow you to sort quickly.

1. Does the firm know your sector? Ask if it follows other businesses in your trade and if it has a dedicated team.
2. Who will be your contact? A name, an office, a meeting frequency, this is what makes the quality of long-term follow-up.
3. Which services are included? Check in the engagement letter what is part of the package (annual accounts, declarations, payroll) and what is a supplementary service (legal advice, forecast, transmission). These landmarks complement the general criteria for [choosing your chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).
4. Can the firm support you as you grow? Hiring, financing, a second location or acquisition of another business demand skills that not all firms have in-house.
5. What tools are offered? An online portal avoids exchanging documents by email and provides real-time visibility of your activity.
