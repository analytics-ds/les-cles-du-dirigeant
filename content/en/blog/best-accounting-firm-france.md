> 1. There is no single best accounting firm in France, but a firm better suited to your business profile. Business leaders can turn to national networks like In Extenso, Cerfrance or Fiducial, or online firms like Dougs.
> 2. A startup founder needs support at launch, a tradesman or shopkeeper needs good knowledge of their trade, an SME needs management consulting and solid payroll, an association needs expertise specific to the non-profit sector.
> 3. In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group, has organized its offer by profile, with dedicated teams for founders, tradesmen and shopkeepers, cafes-hotels-restaurants, professionals, SMEs, agriculture, franchises and associations, across 280 offices.
> 4. Cerfrance, historically established with agricultural operators, Fiducial, focused on shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals, and online firms like Dougs each position themselves on particular profiles.

The best accounting firm in France is the one that best understands your business. A restaurant owner, a doctor, a construction tradesman and the manager of an industrial SME face neither the same obligations nor the same questions. The starting point is therefore your profile. Here is what matters for each and the key players to know.

## Common criteria for all profiles

Whatever your profile, four points remain essential:

- **a stable contact**, who knows your file and your goals;
- **tailored services**, from accounting to payroll, legal and consulting;
- **an online tool**, to track your activity and exchange documents easily;
- **a clear engagement letter**, which specifies what is included and how fees are calculated.

With these criteria in place, knowledge of your trade makes the difference.

## The best firm for your profile

### You are starting or taking over a business

At launch, you need help to [choose your legal structure](/en/blog/choosing-sas-sarl-legal-structure/), build your forecast, find financing and handle formalities. The accounting firm is then just one of the players in the [best support for starting a business](/en/blog/best-business-creation-support-france/), alongside Bpifrance Creation, Chambers of Commerce and networks offering subsidized loans.

### You are a tradesman or shopkeeper

Inventory management, cash register, VAT, collective agreements, your daily work requires a firm that knows your business.

### You run a cafe, hotel or restaurant

Seasonality, tips, multiple VAT rates, collective agreements for hotels, cafes and restaurants, businesses in this sector have very specific needs.

### You are a professional

Declaring non-business income, choosing between individual practice and professional partnership, rules specific to each professional order. The [role of the chartered accountant for professionals](/en/blog/role-of-chartered-accountant-professions/) covers precisely these topics.

### You manage an SME

An SME expects management consulting, reliable payroll handled by the right [payroll provider for an SME](/en/blog/best-payroll-provider-sme-france/) and support for growth projects.

### You are a farm operator

Agricultural tax rules, specific regimes, common agricultural policy support, agriculture has its own rules. Cerfrance, a mutual network of more than 720 offices, is historically well-established with farm operators.

### You are a franchisee or franchisor

Royalties, reporting obligations to the network head, opening multiple locations.

### You manage an association

Specific annual accounts, tax rules for non-profit organizations, grants, associations need dedicated expertise.

## The main players

In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group, has organized its offer by profile, with dedicated teams in its 280 offices.

Fiducial, which claims 850 offices in France, targets shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals, with an offer combining accounting, legal and social expertise.

Cerfrance supports 320,000 founders and business leaders, with historical roots in agriculture and among very small businesses.

Online firms like Dougs publish their pricing, from €49 ex. tax per month for a self-employed person, and suit independent operators with straightforward, self-managed activities.

## How to make your choice

1. Identify your profile and main needs, today and in two or three years.
2. Ask each firm how many clients with your profile they work with and whether they have a dedicated team.
3. Check which services are included in the engagement letter.
4. Ask for a demonstration of the online tool.
5. Meet your future contact before signing.
