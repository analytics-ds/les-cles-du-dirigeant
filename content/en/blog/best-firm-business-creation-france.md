Starting a business is much more than just registration. Before formalities come choosing a legal structure, building a forecast that will convince your banker and identifying available support. After registration comes accounting management, VAT declaration, social contributions and preparing the first balance sheet. The firm supporting your business creation plays a key role in these three phases, which is why choosing well matters.

## What an accounting firm brings to business creation

A chartered accountant intervenes across three key phases. This firm is part of a broader path, described in [best support for starting a business](/en/blog/best-business-creation-support-france/).

- **before registration**, choice of legal structure (individual enterprise, EURL, SARL, SASU or SAS), tax and social options, financial forecast and financing file. The comparison [SAS or SARL](/en/blog/choosing-sas-sarl-legal-structure/) helps you decide between the two most common forms.
- **at registration**, drafting bylaws and formalities with the single window;
- **after registration**, setting up accounting, filing schedule and first management dashboards.

This continuity makes the difference. Decisions made at the start, on legal structure, founder compensation or VAT regime, produce their effects from day one.

## How to choose your firm for business creation

### Consider your project complexity

A simple activity, run alone, without employees or outside financing, can launch with light support. A multi-partner project, with a bank loan, planned recruitment or business acquisition requires complete support, from forecast to bylaws.

### Check what happens after registration

Ask who will handle your accounting, who will prepare your filings and who will be your contact in year one. This is the period when most questions arise, VAT, contributions, founder compensation.

### Compare on equal terms

Put side by side what is included in each offer, bylaw drafting, forecast, banking file, formalities, accounting setup, and add registry and legal publication fees, due in all cases. For a joint-stock company, see [best firm to set up an SAS in France](/en/blog/best-firm-to-set-up-sas-france/).
