## In Extenso's identity and market position

In Extenso is a French group founded in 1991, owned by its shareholder chartered accountants. The 2026 La Profession Comptable magazine ranking, based on figures as of June 30, 2025, places it first in accounting in France. The group also ranks third in regulated activities combining accounting and statutory audit, and sixth by consolidated revenue among French groups.

In Extenso built itself around small businesses, SMEs, professionals and associations, with growth of nearly 60 percent in revenue over nine years according to its chief executive, Antoine de Riedmatten. It is today France's leading independent accounting group.

## Service offering, by lifecycle stage

In Extenso does not sell a single accounting service; it covers the complete company lifecycle. This breadth is the real differentiator of a generalist network.

### Create or take over

- Support for business creation and takeover, from choice of legal structure to financial forecast. See also the [best support for starting a business](/en/blog/best-business-creation-support-france/).
- Acquisition consulting for the purchase of goodwill or shares.

### Daily management

- Accounting and bookkeeping, statutory accounts filing.
- Social management and payroll, including collective agreements and monthly social declarations. The market is presented in the article on [best payroll provider for an SME](/en/blog/best-payroll-provider-sme-france/).
- Tax and filings, income tax, corporate tax, VAT.
- Legal via In Extenso Attorneys, advice and litigation.
- Statutory audit and statutory audit.
- E-invoicing, with Essential Purchase and Sales modules integrated at no extra cost to the Inexweb portal.
- Corporate social responsibility, ESG reporting and compliance.
- Training management.
- Support for finance departments, for businesses with an internal accounting function.

### Develop and structure

- Management and control, dashboards, monthly and annual performance tracking.
- Owner wealth management, handled by In Extenso Wealth Management, a dedicated and independent entity.
- Innovation consulting, notably access to R&D financing schemes.
- Risk management and prevention consulting.
- Restructuring consulting for businesses in difficulty.
- Financing, bank file setup and financing research.
- International growth, for French businesses expanding outside France.
- France implantation, for foreign businesses arriving in France.

### Exit or succeed

- Business sale, valuation and negotiation advice.
- Business acquisition.
- Paid or gift succession.

A business leader planning a sale in three years, who must choose between salary and dividends, and who needs statutory audit, works with one group instead of coordinating three different providers who do not communicate.

## Inexweb, the client platform

Inexweb is the interface between the business and its In Extenso office. It centralizes accounting documents, real-time management tracking and communication with the office adviser.

The notable point is that Inexweb is not a single tool but a platform declined by trade. Inexweb construction, Inexweb automotive, Inexweb shopkeeper, Inexweb wholesale and Inexweb retail each embed metrics specific to the sector.

A garage operator tracks different ratios than a parts wholesaler. This sector breakdown enables every business leader to track metrics specific to their activity.

## The group's specialized entities

In Extenso operates as a network of entities, explaining the depth of support on topics a generalist firm typically outsources.

- In Extenso Wealth Management, for owner wealth management;
- In Extenso Attorneys, for legal advice;
- In Extenso Social, for social management;
- In Extenso Finance, for financial operations;
- In Extenso Innovation Growth, for innovation consulting;
- In Extenso Social and Solidarity Economy, for associations and social economy structures;
- In Extenso Tourism, Culture and Hospitality, for players in these sectors;
- Money Flow, specialized in debt collection, and Caneva, expert in public procurement, which joined the group;
- Ad Astra, operational support offering for finance directors of large accounts and mid-market firms;
- fulll, software for chartered accountants, and Transaxio Hotel, partner of In Extenso.

Operating as a network of entities presents a practical advantage for In Extenso. When a business leader prepares a succession, the wealth team speaks with the accounting and legal teams of the same group. Files do not get lost among providers.

## Business profiles supported

In Extenso organizes its approach by profile, meaning each segment benefits from tailored teams and tools.

The profiles followed are agriculture, tradesmen and shopkeepers, construction, cafes, hotels and restaurants, business creators and takers, works councils, social economy, foreign businesses, franchisors and franchisees, SMEs, professionals, sports clubs and athletes, and startups. The group alone claims over 40,000 tradesman and shopkeeper clients.

The profile-based approach matters more than it appears for In Extenso. What business leaders expect from a firm, beyond accounting technique, is that it knows their trade. A contact supporting multiple restaurants understands seasonality, typical payroll and sector-specific challenges. This is a real advantage of the network model.

## When In Extenso is relevant

Based on the offering structure, several business profiles find real value with In Extenso.

**An SME with employees.** Once there is payroll, a collective agreement to apply and monthly social declarations, the value of a structured network becomes obvious. The group produces over 245,000 payslips each month.

**A multi-location business or growing company.** Territory coverage allows you to have a contact in each region. A business leader opening a new site needs a contact who knows both head office and the new location.

**A business leader planning succession or sale.** Having the wealth adviser, legal adviser and financial adviser in the same group reduces back-and-forth and ensures file consistency.

**An activity with marked sector challenges.** Construction, automotive, commerce, hospitality, agriculture, each with dedicated tools and contacts. In Extenso has invested in understanding these trades.

**A foreign business implanting in France or a French business growing internationally.** The group has a dedicated offering and experienced teams on these topics.

## For whom In Extenso is a coherent answer

In Extenso is a coherent choice once the need goes beyond accounting alone, whether payroll, growth project, succession or acquisition to prepare. For a founder, In Extenso Creation Takeover line also supports the project from idea, with a 10-step method.

In Extenso offers a preliminary diagnosis for e-invoicing enabling assessment of the business's preparation level with no prior commitment.
