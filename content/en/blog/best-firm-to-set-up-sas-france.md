The best firm to set up an SAS in France is one that can both draft customized articles of association and support the company after registration. The SAS is today one of the most chosen company forms by founders, particularly because it adapts to almost all projects. This flexibility comes with a tradeoff. The articles of association do not simply repeat a legal model; they organize power, shareholder entry and exit, and management compensation. Well-drafted articles at the start avoid costly blockages years later. Here is what a firm brings to SAS creation and how to choose one. Registration ends with obtaining the [company registration certificate (Kbis)](/en/blog/company-registration-kbis/).

## Why create an SAS with a firm

### Customized articles of association

In an SAS, the law gives shareholders considerable freedom to organize the company. The articles of association notably define:

- The powers of the president and, where applicable, a chief executive officer or committee;
- Voting rules and decisions that fall to shareholders;
- Approval clauses, pre-emptive rights or inalienability clauses that control entry of new shareholders;
- Conditions for a shareholder's exit.

This freedom precisely justifies professional support. Standard articles work for a simple SASU, much less so for an SAS with multiple founders or expected to welcome investors.

### The shareholders agreement

Alongside the articles of association, which are public, shareholders often sign a shareholders agreement, which is confidential. It organizes relations between founders, role allocation, each person's commitments and exit scenarios. Its drafting requires deep knowledge of the project and its prospects.

### President compensation

The SAS president has employee status if compensated. They benefit from the general Social Security regime, except for unemployment insurance, and their contributions are generally higher than those of an SARL majority manager, who falls under the self-employed worker regime. Dividends paid by an SAS are not subject to social contributions, which opens an option between salary and dividends. This option depends on your personal situation and is built with professional advice. The comparison [SAS or SARL](/en/blog/choosing-sas-sarl-legal-structure/) details these differences in legal status.

### Creation formalities

Creating an SAS follows several steps: drafting and signing the articles of association, deposit of capital contributions, publication of a legal notice, then declaration to the unified business formalities portal. The SIREN number is then assigned by INSEE and the company is registered in the commercial and corporate registry. Capital is freely set by the articles of association.

## How to choose your firm for creating an SAS

1. **Evaluate project complexity.** A simple SASU with no employees is created with light support. An SAS with multiple shareholders, a shareholders agreement, in-kind contributions or planned fundraising requires real legal work.
2. **Check who drafts the articles.** A lawyer or chartered accountant who adapts articles to your project, or a template filled in online.
3. **Plan for president compensation.** Ask for a simulation of what each option costs and brings in, salary, dividends or a combination of both.
4. **Think about afterward.** The firm creating your SAS must be able to handle accounting, process payroll and ensure annual legal secretarial work. This is also a key point in the [best support for creating your business](/en/blog/best-business-creation-support-france/).

<summary>What is the difference between an SAS and a SASU?</summary>

A SASU is an SAS with only one shareholder. Operating rules are the same, and the entry of a new shareholder transforms the SASU into an SAS, with updated articles of association.
