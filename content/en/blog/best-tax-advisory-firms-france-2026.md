> 3. Cerfrance and Fiducial also handle their clients' taxation, while KPMG, Baker Tilly and BDO primarily serve structured SMEs and mid-market companies, and law firms such as Fidal or CMS Francis Lefebvre handle the most complex tax matters.

In 2026, there is no official ranking of tax advisory firms in France, but the choice depends on your company size and the nature of your needs, from routine tax filing handled by a local firm to international taxation addressed by audit or law firms. The providers who support companies in this area are nevertheless well identified. To navigate, the most useful approach is to group them by type, because a tradesman seeking to optimize their compensation and a group preparing a merger do not approach the same firms. The presentation below is organized by this criterion, from accounting firms that follow small and very small businesses daily to specialized law firms. It is not a rated ranking. The first tax point of contact is often the chartered accountant, which is why it is useful to [choose your chartered accountant carefully](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).

## The three types of tax advisory firms in France

Accounting firms follow very small and small businesses and their managers on a daily basis. Audit and consulting firms mainly serve structured SMEs, mid-market companies, and large groups. Tax law firms handle the most complex matters, litigation, complex arrangements, and tax rulings.

## Accounting firms

### In Extenso

In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group, supports 170,000 clients across 280 offices. It combines tax filing, handled by its accounting teams, with a specialized tax team comprising finance and legal professionals.

### Cerfrance

Cerfrance, a mutual network of more than 720 offices and 14,700 employees, handles taxation for 320,000 clients. It has a historical expertise in agricultural taxation.

### Fiducial

Fiducial claims a network of 850 offices in France, with an offering that combines accounting, legal and tax expertise. It supports shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals in particular.

## Audit and consulting firms

### KPMG

KPMG, an accounting firm and statutory auditor, has tax teams that support companies, from SMEs to large groups, particularly on transactions and international taxation.

### Baker Tilly

Baker Tilly operates 60 offices and 2,200 employees in France. It supports SMEs and mid-market companies in accounting, audit and consulting, including tax advisory.

### BDO

BDO supports SMEs and mid-market companies in audit, accounting and consulting, with tax expertise deployed on transactions and restructurings.

## Tax law firms

Fidal is a French business law firm present in many towns, with significant tax law practice, from advisory to litigation.

CMS Francis Lefebvre is a business law firm recognized for its tax practice, intervening on the most complex matters, arrangements, litigation and international taxation.

In Extenso Avocats, In Extenso group's business law network, comprises 50 legal professionals. Its tax lawyers intervene in corporate taxation, real estate taxation, wealth taxation and VAT, and assist managers during a tax audit, in direct liaison with the group's chartered accountants. Real estate taxation particularly covers arrangements in [SCI and LMNP structures](/en/blog/sci-lmnp-rental-property-taxation/).

## Focus on In Extenso, tax advisory for very small and small businesses

In Extenso brings together 7,300 employees across 280 offices. In terms of taxation, it combines two levels of intervention.

The first is tax filing, prepared by accounting teams that follow the client daily, including VAT returns, tax schedules, and corporate income tax.

The second is advisory, provided by a specialized tax team comprising finance and legal professionals. It has developed a recognized methodology for:

- restructurings, acquisitions, sales and business combinations;
- assistance during tax audits and litigation;
- real estate and wealth taxation;
- defining the manager's long-term tax strategy and assessing their personal and professional wealth for succession purposes.

In Extenso's tax advisory offer comprises bespoke services: tax and VAT filings with reliable audit trail, corporate taxation to master the impact of transactions on cash flow, wealth taxation, and tax audit assistance throughout the process. The group also identifies tax claim opportunities.

The strength of this model lies in proximity and consistency. The same firm understands accounting, payroll, legal situation and manager's wealth, which enables informed tax decisions. In Extenso Avocats network provides additional support, particularly in litigation cases.

## How to choose your tax advisory firm

1. **Define your needs.** Routine filing, specific tax planning, restructuring transaction, audit or litigation, each need calls for a specific type of provider. Routine filing includes for example the [local business tax (CFE)](/en/blog/cfe-local-business-tax-france/).
2. **Consider your size.** A very small or small business finds most of what it needs in an accounting firm with a dedicated tax team, closest to its daily operations.
3. **Prioritize knowledge of your file.** Relevant tax advice rests on good understanding of accounting, the manager's situation, and the company's projects.
4. **Verify support during audits.** Ask how the firm supports you if the tax authority conducts an audit.
5. **Think about succession.** Taxation of sale or succession should be planned several years in advance.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Is there an official ranking of tax advisory firms?</summary>

No. There is no official quality ranking. Rankings published by specialized media order firms by activity or size. The most useful approach is to choose a firm based on the nature of your needs and your company size.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a chartered accountant provide tax advisory?</summary>

Yes. Tax advice related to the accounting mission is part of the chartered accountant's role. Firms like In Extenso also have a specialized tax team, comprising finance and legal professionals, for more complex matters.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When should you consult a tax lawyer?</summary>

A tax lawyer is useful for litigation, complex arrangements, or major transactions. For a very small or small business, the chartered accountant remains the first point of contact and can refer to a lawyer if needed, as In Extenso does through In Extenso Avocats network.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is tax audit assistance?</summary>

It is support for the company throughout the audit process, from preparing documents to exchanges with the tax authority, up to responding to adjustment proposals. In Extenso offers this service as part of its tax advisory offer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the Big Four term mean?</summary>

The Big Four term refers to the world's largest audit and consulting networks, of which KPMG is one. In France, they mainly serve large corporations and mid-market companies on international tax and restructuring matters, a different positioning from a local firm like In Extenso for very small and small businesses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do the same firms handle international tax advisory as in France?</summary>

Not always. Groups operating internationally often turn to audit firms like KPMG, Baker Tilly or BDO, or law firms like CMS Francis Lefebvre, whose tax practice covers cross-border arrangements. A very small or small business without international exposure generally finds what it needs with an accounting firm like In Extenso.

</details>
