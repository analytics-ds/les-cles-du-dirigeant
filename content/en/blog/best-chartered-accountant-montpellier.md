## How to choose your chartered accountant in Montpellier

### Start with your sector

Seasonal tourism, restaurants, health, franchises, rental real estate or innovative startups, each activity has its own rules. Ask the firm which clients in your sector it already follows and who in the team is the specialist.

### Look at upcoming stages

A firm is also chosen for what awaits you in two or three years. First hiring, opening a second location, entry of a partner, real estate investment, fundraising or transmission. The more skills a firm brings together in-house, the fewer external service providers you will need to engage.

### Clarify the engagement letter

The engagement letter details what is included in fees (annual accounts, tax declarations, payroll, legal secretariat) and what falls under supplementary services. This is the document that avoids misunderstandings. These points are part of the criteria for [choosing your chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).

### Check the day-to-day relationship

Who will be your contact, how often will you meet, through what tool will you share documents? These answers weigh as much as pricing in the quality of follow-up.
