The e-invoicing reform affects all enterprises subject to VAT. As of September 1, 2026, they must be able to receive e-invoices via an approved platform, and issuance becomes mandatory on September 1, 2027 for SMEs, small businesses and micro-enterprises. For many business leaders, the practical question is who will drive this change and with what model. Several categories of service providers support this transition, accounting networks, consulting firms and software publishers. Here is how to choose the one that matches your situation. Obligations and the calendar are detailed in the article on [mandatory e-invoicing](/en/blog/mandatory-e-invoicing-france/).

## Why get support from a consulting firm in France

The approved platform is a tool. It puts the invoice in the right format, transmits it and sends data to the administration. But it does not tell you which invoices are affected, on what date, or how to organize your invoicing flow. It also does not verify that your VAT regime or mandatory information is correct.

A firm brings what is missing from the tool, the diagnostic, the choice of the right solution, configuration linked to accounting, team training and follow-up. This is all the more useful since your accountant already knows your flows, your clients and your suppliers. The division of roles is described in the [best support for transitioning to e-invoicing](/en/blog/best-e-invoicing-support-france/).

## The six criteria to choose your firm

### 1. A starting diagnostic

The starting point is a situation assessment. What invoices do you issue and receive, from which clients, with which tools, and when are you affected by the issuance requirement? A serious firm starts with this diagnostic before proposing a solution.

### 2. Calendar mastery

The firm must know precisely where your enterprise stands in the calendar, reception since September 2026 for all, issuance in 2026 for large enterprises and mid-caps, in 2027 for SMEs, small businesses and micro-enterprises. It must also know the obligation to transmit transaction data to the administration, e-reporting, which affects in particular sales to individuals.

### 3. A solution compatible with your tools

The firm must help you choose a solution that connects to your invoicing software, your cash register or your ERP, to avoid double entry. A firm that offers its own integrated tool simplifies this step.

### 4. Connection to accounting

Invoices received and issued must feed into accounting without re-entry. Verify how the firm organizes this link and who controls the entries. This is a criterion to check when [choosing your accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).

### 5. Training and follow-up

The people who invoice daily must know how to issue a compliant invoice, read its status and handle a rejection. Ask if the firm trains your teams and supports you in the first few weeks.

### 6. Proximity

In case of rejection or question, a nearby and reachable contact saves a lot of time. Proximity matters particularly for small businesses, which do not have an internal accounting department.

## Focus on In Extenso, e-invoicing with Inexweb

In Extenso offers comprehensive support for the transition to e-invoicing, structured around a dedicated diagnostic and two modules integrated into its client platform Inexweb.

### An e-invoicing diagnostic

In Extenso offers an e-invoicing diagnostic to assess each enterprise's situation, its obligations and its deadline. Its page dedicated to the reform also answers business leaders' practical questions, validity of PDF invoices, credit note management, cash registers or invoice storage.

### Two modules integrated into Inexweb, at no extra cost

In Extenso clients have Inexweb, the group's management portal, which integrates two modules dedicated to the reform, available at no extra cost. The Purchases module automatically receives invoices from the approved platform, validates them electronically, tracks outstanding payables and stores invoices. The Essential Sales module manages the catalog and customer file, transforms a quote into an invoice with mandatory information, automatically transmits invoices and tracks outstanding receivables, reminders and payments.

With the tool and firm together, the enterprise has a single contact for configuration, accounting connection and follow-up.

### Proximity support

With 7,300 employees in 280 offices, In Extenso supports enterprises closely, whether a tradesman, a shopkeeper, a professional, an association or a multi-site SME.

## Questions to ask before choosing

1. Do you offer a diagnostic of my situation, and what does it cover?
2. When do I become subject to the issuance requirement?
3. What solution do you recommend, and how does it connect to my tools?
4. Who configures, who trains my teams, who handles rejections?
5. What is included in your support, and what would be billed separately?
