In a small business, the owner often does everything, production, sales, management. Time spent on administration is time lost from the core business. That is why choosing the right chartered accountant matters so much. A good firm frees the owner from accounting, social and tax obligations, and helps him make sound decisions. Here is what to expect from the best chartered accountant for a small business in France and the key providers to know.

## What a small business in France expects from its chartered accountant

A small business has concrete needs:

- accounting bookkeeping or review and preparation of annual accounts;
- tax declarations, VAT, profit tax, [local business tax (CFE)](/en/blog/cfe-local-business-tax-france/);
- payroll and social declarations from the first employee, to be delegated if needed to a [payroll provider for SMEs](/en/blog/best-payroll-provider-sme-france/);
- annual legal administration for corporations;
- advice on owner compensation, legal structure, cash flow and investments;
- compliance with e-invoicing, mandatory for receipt from 1 September 2026 and issuance from 1 September 2027 for small businesses.

A small business also needs proximity and responsiveness. A question about an employment contract or a letter from the administration calls for a quick answer, from someone who knows the file.

## How to choose your small business's chartered accountant

1. **Check that they understand your industry.** A firm that supports other businesses in your sector knows its rules and what to watch for. This criterion aligns with those detailed for [choosing your chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).
2. **Ask who will be your point of contact.** Continuity matters more in a small business, where the owner has no administrative team to liaise.
3. **List the services you need.** Payroll, legal, VAT, advice, what you won't handle yourself must be covered.
4. **Think about what comes next.** Your first employee, buying a premises, a partner joining. Choose a firm that can support you at that stage.
5. **Compare engagement letters on equal scope.** Price only makes sense in relation to what is included.
