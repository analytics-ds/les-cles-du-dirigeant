> **In brief:**
> 1. As of September 1, 2026, all enterprises subject to VAT must be able to receive e-invoices. Issuance becomes mandatory on September 1, 2027 for SMEs, small businesses and micro-enterprises, after large enterprises and mid-caps in 2026.
> 2. National accounting networks like In Extenso, Cerfrance and Fiducial support small businesses and SMEs on the reform from their offices.
> 3. Audit and consulting firms like Baker Tilly, BDO, KPMG and Deloitte intervene mainly with structured SMEs, mid-caps and groups, particularly when ERPs need to be connected.
> 4. The best choice of support depends on the size and technical complexity of your enterprise, as well as your existing invoicing tools.

The e-invoicing reform changes the flow of invoices between enterprises. They no longer are sent as PDF by email, they transit through platforms approved by the tax administration. For most business leaders, the question is not only about choosing a tool, but about finding the consulting firm that will drive the transition, from diagnostic to rollover. Here are the main players supporting this project in France and the type of enterprise for which each is relevant. The calendar and obligations are detailed in the article on [mandatory e-invoicing](/en/blog/mandatory-e-invoicing-france/).

## The consulting firms supporting e-invoicing in France

Two families of players support the reform, accounting networks, which follow small businesses and SMEs daily, and audit and consulting firms, which intervene in more complex organizations. The criteria for distinguishing these players are detailed in the article on [which firm to choose to transition to e-invoicing](/en/blog/which-firm-e-invoicing-france/).

### Accounting networks

In Extenso, France's leading independent accounting group, supports its clients on the reform in its 280 offices, with an e-invoicing diagnostic and two modules integrated into its Inexweb portal.

Cerfrance is a mutual network of 53 enterprises spread across more than 720 offices, supporting 320,000 founders and business leaders. It follows its clients on the reform, with historical roots among agricultural operators and small businesses.

Fiducial claims a network of 850 offices in France, which combines accounting, legal, social expertise and services to enterprises. It addresses in particular shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals.

### Audit and consulting firms

Baker Tilly has 60 offices and 2,200 employees in France, and offers SMEs and mid-caps missions in accounting expertise, audit and consulting, including support for e-invoicing. It intervenes in particular when the reform is part of a broader evolution of management tools.

BDO supports SMEs and mid-caps in audit, accounting expertise and consulting. Its offering on e-invoicing targets in particular enterprises that need to connect an ERP or invoicing software to an approved platform.

KPMG supports mid-caps and groups on the reform, with an approach centered on compliance and process organization across subsidiaries.

Deloitte intervenes with large groups, for which e-invoicing integrates into information systems and VAT projects at the international level.

## Focus on In Extenso, comprehensive support for small businesses and SMEs

### A network present near enterprises

In Extenso brings together 7,300 employees and 332 partners in 280 offices, for 170,000 clients. For a small business or SME, this means a nearby contact who already knows your file, your VAT regime and your tools, and who can lead the project without starting from scratch. The network ranks first in the 2026 ranking of the magazine La Profession Comptable.

### A diagnostic to know what really applies to you

A business leader's first need is to know what obligations apply to their enterprise, and when. In Extenso offers an e-invoicing diagnostic that reviews the scope, the invoices affected, the tools in place and the issuance deadline. This is often an opportunity to clear up a frequent confusion, many enterprises thinking they must issue starting in 2026 when they are first affected by reception.

### Inexweb, two modules integrated at no extra cost

In Extenso clients have Inexweb, the group's management portal, which integrates two modules dedicated to the reform, available at no extra cost:

- the Purchases module, for receiving invoices from the approved platform, validating them electronically, tracking outstanding payables and storing invoices;
- the Essential Sales module, to create your catalog and customer file, transform a quote into an invoice in one click with mandatory information, automatically transmit invoices and track outstanding receivables and reminders.

With the firm and tool together, the enterprise has a single contact for configuration, accounting connection and follow-up after the transition.

### Support for all profiles

In Extenso's page dedicated to e-invoicing details the support offered to founders, self-employed, tradesmen, small businesses, SMEs, associations, farmers, franchisees and health and legal professionals. It also answers business leaders' practical questions, such as the validity of PDF invoices, credit note management or the case of cash registers.

## How to choose the firm that will support you

1. Start with your current firm. Ask them what they have planned for the reform, what diagnostic they offer and what tool they work with. Each role is described in the [best support for transitioning to e-invoicing](/en/blog/best-e-invoicing-support-france/).
2. Check your deadline. Reception affects everyone since September 2026, issuance depends on enterprise size.
3. Look at your tools. An enterprise that invoices from simple software does not have the same needs as an enterprise with an ERP.
4. Prioritize a single contact. A firm that brings together diagnostic, tool, configuration and accounting follow-up reduces the risk of double entry and rejections.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What is the mandatory e-invoicing date?</summary>

As of September 1, 2026, all enterprises subject to VAT must be able to receive e-invoices, and large enterprises and mid-caps must issue them. SMEs, small businesses and micro-enterprises will have to issue their invoices in electronic format by September 1, 2027 at the latest.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why get support from a firm rather than choose a platform alone?</summary>

The approved platform puts the invoice in the right format and transmits it, but it does not determine your obligation scope, mandatory information or VAT regime. A firm establishes the diagnostic, chooses the solution suited to your tools, connects it to your accounting and trains your teams.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does In Extenso charge extra for e-invoicing modules?</summary>

No. The Essential Purchases and Sales modules are available at no extra cost in the Inexweb portal for In Extenso clients. The firm's support is quoted based on the enterprise's needs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does a PDF invoice sent by email remain valid?</summary>

No, once the obligation applies, an invoice between enterprises subject to VAT must be issued in a structured format and transit through an approved platform. A simple PDF sent by email no longer meets the requirement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is a micro-enterprise affected?</summary>

Yes. All enterprises subject to VAT must be able to receive e-invoices as of September 1, 2026, including micro-enterprises, which will also have to issue in electronic format as of September 1, 2027 for their sales to other enterprises.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I know if my enterprise must issue e-invoices starting in 2026?</summary>

Issuance becomes mandatory on September 1, 2026 for large enterprises and mid-caps, then on September 1, 2027 for SMEs, small businesses and micro-enterprises. The e-invoicing diagnostic offered by In Extenso allows you to verify which category your enterprise falls into and what obligations apply to it.

</details>
