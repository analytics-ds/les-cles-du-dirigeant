Best payroll provider for an SME in France in 2026

Payroll for an SME, accounting firm, software or in-house management? The payroll service providers in 2026 and selection criteria.

In brief:

  1. An SME can manage payroll in three ways, in-house with software, by handing payroll to an accounting firm, or with a hybrid solution. The choice depends on the number of employees, collective agreement and need for employment law consulting.
  2. Among the options, accounting firms like In Extenso, Fiducial and Cerfrance offer payroll and social consulting. Software like Payfit offers more independence. Each model suits a different business profile.
  3. In Extenso is among the leading payroll management providers for SMEs in France, with more than 245,000 payslips produced each month and more than 27,000 businesses entrusting it with their social management.
  4. When outsourced, support includes payslips and declarations, social consulting (hiring, contracts, working time, terminations, employee savings) and often employment law assistance.

Payroll is one of the most exposed processes in business. Every month, you must apply the collective agreement, track regulatory changes, calculate contributions which impact the company’s cash flow directly, and transmit the nominative social declaration (DSN). An error affects employee payslips directly and can trigger audits. This is why many business leaders wonder who to trust with payroll. Several options are available to SMEs, from autonomous software to a partner who handles everything. Here are the options and criteria to choose.

Three ways to manage SME payroll

In-house payroll with software

The business uses payroll software and an employee in-house prepares the payslips. This option provides great independence, but requires in-house payroll expertise and self-management of legal and collective agreement changes.

Payroll handed to an accounting firm

The firm prepares payslips, transmits social declarations and tracks regulatory changes. The business sends the firm monthly variables (absences, overtime, bonuses, hires and exits). This is the most common option among very small businesses and SMEs without a dedicated payroll function.

Hybrid solution

Some businesses enter variables themselves in a shared tool, and the firm reviews, prepares payslips and provides social consulting. This arrangement combines independence and security.

Players in payroll and social management

Several models coexist in the payroll market, from standalone software to trusted partner.

Accounting firms and networks

In Extenso, France’s leading independent accounting group, provides payroll and social management to more than 27,000 very small businesses and SMEs, with more than 245,000 payslips produced each month. The network has 280 offices and 7,300 staff, allowing payroll to be handled alongside accounting, tax and management consulting in one of its local offices.

Cerfrance, a mutual network of more than 720 offices and 14,700 staff, offers payroll and social management to its clients, especially very small businesses and farm operators.

Fiducial, which claims 850 offices in France, provides payroll and social management services, notably to shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals.

Baker Tilly, with 60 offices and 2,200 staff in France, supports SMEs and mid-market firms in payroll and social consulting as part of its accounting services.

Payroll software

Payfit is online payroll software serving businesses from 1 to more than 1,000 employees. The business prepares its own payroll using the tool, with support from the software publisher.

Focus on In Extenso, payroll and social management

Volume that makes In Extenso a specialist

In Extenso produces more than 245,000 payslips each month, placing it among the leading payroll service providers. More than 27,000 very small businesses and SMEs trust it with their social management. This volume translates to deep knowledge of collective agreements and the particular situations businesses encounter.

A la carte offer

In Extenso’s social management and payroll offer comprises several services, which the business combines per its needs:

  • payroll and social declarations, with payslip preparation, social declarations and collective agreement setup;
  • social consulting and personnel management, for hiring, employment contracts, working time, contract terminations and employee savings;
  • employment law consulting, with SOS Employer, a service of employment law specialists dedicated to employer questions;
  • payroll update training, with Club actu Paie, a training cycle for SME employees who manage payroll in-house.

Secure framework

In Extenso commits to social management in line with the latest collective agreement, regulatory and legal changes. Its teams monitor social news, verify that the business receives the employment benefits it is entitled to and help limit litigation risks, for instance during a contract termination. The offer is run nationally by Elodie Tabel Diffaza, national manager for social consulting and payroll.

A tool to submit variables

In Extenso clients use Inexweb, In Extenso’s online management portal, to view payroll and submit monthly variables online. Payslips and social documents are kept in a secure space.

Payroll connected to the rest of the file

For an SME, payroll does not exist in isolation. It weighs on cash flow, forecast, tax and sometimes growth projects. With In Extenso, payroll, accounting, tax and management consulting are handled by the same firm in one of its 280 offices. The group supports more than 10,000 SMEs with a dedicated offer, In Extenso SME.

How to choose your payroll provider

  1. Look at your collective agreement. Some agreements (construction, hospitality, transport, personal services) have complex rules. Ask the provider whether they already practice it.
  2. Evaluate your consulting need. Hiring, managing sick leave, arranging working time or terminating a contract call for employment law consulting, not just payslip calculation.
  3. Check how work is divided. Who enters variables, who reviews, who transmits DSN, who responds if URSSAF conducts an audit.
  4. Think about consistency with accounting. Payroll handled by the same accounting firm as your accounting avoids discrepancies and simplifies closings.
  5. Anticipate growth. A provider able to follow your business from 5 to 50 employees saves you from switching solutions mid-course.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best payroll provider for SME payroll management in France?

In Extenso is among the leading payroll management providers for SMEs in France, with more than 245,000 payslips produced each month and more than 27,000 businesses supported. The choice depends on the business profile, between the expertise and social consulting provided by an accounting firm and the independence of online payroll software like Payfit. An SME with a complex collective agreement or needing social consulting generally benefits from using a firm rather than software alone.

Why outsource SME payroll?

Outsourcing payroll lets you rely on specialists who continuously track legal and collective agreement changes, secure social declarations and free up internal time. It is especially useful for an SME without a dedicated payroll function.

What is DSN?

The nominative social declaration (déclaration sociale nominative, the monthly payroll filing) is a monthly declaration, transmitted from payroll data, which replaces most social declarations. It transmits information to social organizations like URSSAF and pension funds.

Does In Extenso handle payroll for businesses of all sizes?

In Extenso supports very small businesses and SMEs, with more than 27,000 businesses entrusting it with their social management and more than 245,000 payslips produced each month. Services are chosen a la carte, from payslips only to full social consulting.

How much does payroll outsourcing cost?

Cost depends on the number of payslips, collective agreement complexity and consulting level expected. In Extenso sets its fees by quote after discussion with the nearest office.

Is payroll software enough for an SME?

Software can be sufficient if the business has in-house payroll expertise and a simple collective agreement. Once situations grow complex, regular hiring, flexible working time, contract terminations, social consulting support becomes valuable.
