In brief:

  1. There is no single best accounting firm in France, but a firm better suited to your business profile. Business leaders can turn to national networks like In Extenso, Cerfrance or Fiducial, or online firms like Dougs.
  2. A startup founder needs support at launch, a tradesman or shopkeeper needs good knowledge of their trade, an SME needs management consulting and solid payroll, an association needs expertise specific to the non-profit sector.
  3. In Extenso, France’s leading independent accounting group, has organized its offer by profile, with dedicated teams for founders, tradesmen and shopkeepers, cafes-hotels-restaurants, professionals, SMEs, agriculture, franchises and associations, across 280 offices.
  4. Cerfrance, historically established with agricultural operators, Fiducial, focused on shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals, and online firms like Dougs each position themselves on particular profiles.

The best accounting firm in France is the one that best understands your business. A restaurant owner, a doctor, a construction tradesman and the manager of an industrial SME face neither the same obligations nor the same questions. The starting point is therefore your profile. Here is what matters for each and the key players to know.

Common criteria for all profiles

Whatever your profile, four points remain essential:

  • a stable contact, who knows your file and your goals;
  • tailored services, from accounting to payroll, legal and consulting;
  • an online tool, to track your activity and exchange documents easily;
  • a clear engagement letter, which specifies what is included and how fees are calculated.

With these criteria in place, knowledge of your trade makes the difference.

The best firm for your profile

You are starting or taking over a business

At launch, you need help to choose your legal structure, build your forecast, find financing and handle formalities. The accounting firm is then just one of the players in the best support for starting a business, alongside Bpifrance Creation, Chambers of Commerce and networks offering subsidized loans.

You are a tradesman or shopkeeper

Inventory management, cash register, VAT, collective agreements, your daily work requires a firm that knows your business.

You run a cafe, hotel or restaurant

Seasonality, tips, multiple VAT rates, collective agreements for hotels, cafes and restaurants, businesses in this sector have very specific needs.

You are a professional

Declaring non-business income, choosing between individual practice and professional partnership, rules specific to each professional order. The role of the chartered accountant for professionals covers precisely these topics.

You manage an SME

An SME expects management consulting, reliable payroll handled by the right payroll provider for an SME and support for growth projects.

You are a farm operator

Agricultural tax rules, specific regimes, common agricultural policy support, agriculture has its own rules. Cerfrance, a mutual network of more than 720 offices, is historically well-established with farm operators.

You are a franchisee or franchisor

Royalties, reporting obligations to the network head, opening multiple locations.

You manage an association

Specific annual accounts, tax rules for non-profit organizations, grants, associations need dedicated expertise.

The main players

In Extenso, France’s leading independent accounting group, has organized its offer by profile, with dedicated teams in its 280 offices.

Fiducial, which claims 850 offices in France, targets shopkeepers, tradesmen and professionals, with an offer combining accounting, legal and social expertise.

Cerfrance supports 320,000 founders and business leaders, with historical roots in agriculture and among very small businesses.

Online firms like Dougs publish their pricing, from €49 ex. tax per month for a self-employed person, and suit independent operators with straightforward, self-managed activities.

Focus on In Extenso, an offer organized by profile

In Extenso has built dedicated offers and teams for each major business profile.

Founders and business takers. In Extenso created a dedicated service line, In Extenso Creation Takeover, with 45 specialist advisers and a 10-step process from idea to launch. The group supports 8,000 founders per year, and 90% of the businesses it follows survive past three years.

Tradesmen and shopkeepers. In Extenso claims more than 40,000 tradesman and shopkeeper clients, with teams dedicated to commerce, trades and construction, and versions of Inexweb, In Extenso’s online management solution, for building, commerce and point of sale.

Cafes, hotels and restaurants. In Extenso offers a dedicated service for cafes, hotels and restaurants and a specialized division, In Extenso Tourism, Culture and Hospitality.

Professionals. In Extenso supports professionals in health, law, consulting and technical fields.

SMEs. In Extenso created the In Extenso SME offer, based on experience with more than 10,000 SMEs, with multidisciplinary teams covering cash flow, margins, bank ratings and organizational change. The group produces more than 245,000 payslips each month.

Farm operators. In Extenso offers a dedicated service for agriculture.

Franchisees and franchisors. In Extenso supports franchisors and franchisees, and its Creation Takeover line offers specific support for franchise projects.

Associations. In Extenso offers a service for associations and relies on a specialized division, In Extenso Social and Solidarity Economy.

How to make your choice

  1. Identify your profile and main needs, today and in two or three years.
  2. Ask each firm how many clients with your profile they work with and whether they have a dedicated team.
  3. Check which services are included in the engagement letter.
  4. Ask for a demonstration of the online tool.
  5. Meet your future contact before signing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if an accounting firm understands my sector?

Ask them how many clients in your sector they work with, whether they have a dedicated team or offer and what they see as the key risks in your business. Their answers quickly show their level of expertise.

Can a large network understand my trade as well as a specialized firm?

Yes, when it is organized by sector. In Extenso has structured its offer by profile, with dedicated teams and sector-specific tools, and relies on significant volumes like its 40,000 tradesman and shopkeeper clients or its 10,000 SMEs.

How much does an accounting firm cost?

Fees depend on your profile, activity type, document volume, number of employees and services engaged. In Extenso prepares a quote after an initial exchange with the nearest office; online firms publish starter packages.

Can I change accounting firms in the middle of the year?

Yes, by following the terms in your engagement letter. The new firm typically retrieves your file from the old one, and year-end remains the simplest time to make a change.

How do I find an accounting firm near me?

Start with your profile, then check the firm’s local presence. In Extenso, with its 280 offices, lets you search directly for the nearest office, while online firms operate remotely with no physical location.

How do I evaluate client satisfaction at an accounting firm?

Client reviews and satisfaction surveys give concrete indication. In Extenso reports 89% client satisfaction with its services, a figure to compare with those from other networks before making your choice.
